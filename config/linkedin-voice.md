# LinkedIn voice guide (AI Daily Brief)

Feedback, 13 Nov 2026: the posts were "very very busy". They should be in-depth and read as if a person wrote them.

## The shape of a post
- **One story per post.** Never a numbered list of three or four news items. If several stories matter, pick the strongest and save the rest for later slots.
- **About 180–300 words** in short paragraphs of 1–3 sentences, with a blank line between paragraphs.
- **Line 1 is the hook:** a plain statement of the news, or a sharp observation about it. No emoji, no "BREAKING", no clickbait.
- **Lines 2–3:** why it caught the writer's attention. This is the angle, not a summary.
- **Middle:** the context a busy professional lacks. What came before, what is actually new, what it changes, and what is still unknown.
- **Optional:** "Two things I'll be watching" with at most 2–3 short bullets. That is the only list allowed.
- **Close:** one honest line that sums it up, then one specific question to the reader. Avoid generic questions like "What do you think?".
- **Footer:** `AI-assisted, source-checked.`, then 3 hashtags at most.
- **The source link goes on one line, `Source: URL`, just above the footer.** Buffer's free plan refuses first comments.

## Voice
- First person and conversational: "What caught my attention…", "I'd want to know…". Use British English.
- Opinions are framed as reasoning ("that matters because…"). Never present them as facts.
- **Never invent experiences, clients, colleagues, numbers or quotes.** No "I spoke to a CTO last week". Only the user may add a personal story.
- No labels such as "Why it matters:" or "Summary:" (the single `Source:` line above the footer is the only exception), and no "Take:" line.
- Banned filler: "game-changer", "revolutionise", "in today's fast-paced world", "delve", "unlock", "it's important to note", "landscape". No rhetorical triplets (patterns like "faster, smarter, better").
- No Agilisys, customer or personal data, ever.

## Facts
- Every factual claim must trace to the source or a corroborating source checked that day. Keep numbers exact and attributed.
- If a figure rests on one source, say "reported" or drop it.
- Say what is unknown rather than filling the gap.

## The morning carousel caption
- Same voice in 80–150 words: the hook, why the topic matters at work, one "Swipe for…" line, and a question.
