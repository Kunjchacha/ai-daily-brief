Bigger is not always better with AI models. It depends on the job.

It is tempting to reach for the most powerful model every time. But for a lot of everyday work, such as summarising notes, tidying a draft or sorting a list, a smaller model can do the job well, and usually faster and more cheaply.

There is also a neat trick behind many small models. They can be trained to imitate a larger one, a technique called knowledge distillation. You keep much of the skill and lose much of the cost.

Bigger models still earn their place on hard, multi-step problems.
