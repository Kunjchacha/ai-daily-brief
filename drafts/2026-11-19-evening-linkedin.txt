Why it matters: Users can now query Search's AI feature without affecting their personalized profiles or stored history.
Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/googles-ai-feature-in-search-gets-its-own-incognito-mode/

Why it matters: The new hardware allows developers and creators to run and fine-tune larger AI models locally on a PC.
