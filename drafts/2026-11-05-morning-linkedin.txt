Your AI bill is counted in something you never see: tokens.

Models read tokens, not words: whole words, word fragments or punctuation marks.

Limits, speed and price for most AI services are measured in tokens.

The same sentence can use more or fewer tokens on different models.

Takeaway: When a tool quotes a limit or a price, check whether it is counting tokens or words.
