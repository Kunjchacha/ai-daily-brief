Most disappointing AI answers start with a vague request. Ask for "something about our new report" and the tool has to guess four things: who it is for, what shape it should take, how long it should be and what to do when the report is unclear.

The better prompt answers all four before the AI has to guess. It takes ten extra seconds to write and usually saves a round of rewrites.
