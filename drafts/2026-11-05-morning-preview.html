What is a token?
Language models do not read words; they read tokens, which are chunks of text such as a whole word, part of a word or a punctuation mark. Limits, speed and pricing for most AI services are measured in tokens, so the same sentence can use more or fewer tokens depending on the model.

Source: https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/glossary
