A new AI paper is getting attention: Antom 3-in-1 Transformer: A Payment Foundation Model

The authors developed Antom 3-in-1 Transformer, a payment foundation model that combines sequence, tabular, and graph representations and contains about 7 billion parameters, with roughly 1.7 billion activated per task route, and pretrained it on Ant International's internal payment data. They report that pretraining improves a target business metric by 34.5% over training from scratch, that with only 25% of the training labels the pretrained model outperforms a full‑label scratch baseline in Average Precision and Recall@0.5% across merchant domains, and that in a four‑month online experiment the model reduced overall loss rate by 37.88% compared with XGB‑Online.

The authors report: "In a four-month online experiment on Ant International's billion-scale payment platform, the model reduced the overall loss rate by 37.88% relative to XGB-Online."

Takeaway: These are the authors' own claims, and a preprint may not have been peer reviewed yet.
