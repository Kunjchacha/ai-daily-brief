Why does an AI seem to forget the start of a long chat? The context window.

A context window is the amount of text, counted in tokens, that a model can take into account at once.

If a conversation or document is longer than the window, the model cannot use all of it in one request.

Takeaway: For long documents, check the model's window, or split the job into parts.
